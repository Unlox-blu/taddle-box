## 1. Who we are
This privacy policy describes how <Company/Developer Name> ("we", "us") collects,
uses, and shares information when you use the TaddleBox mobile application (the
"App").

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account information** — name, username, email address, and phone number that
  you provide when creating an account or signing in with Google or Apple.
- **Profile content** — your bio, avatar, posts, comments, reposts, and community
  memberships.
- **Location** — when you grant the App location permission, we collect your
  approximate location to attach it to posts you create and to provide
  location-based features. You can grant or deny this permission at any time in
  your device settings.
- **Photos and media** — when you grant photo access, we access images and videos
  you choose to attach to posts or your profile. We never access media you do not
  choose.
- **Device and usage data** — device model, operating system version, app
  version, and how you interact with the App, used to operate and improve the
  service.
- **Payment information** — when you add funds through our payment partner, we
  process the transaction through the partner's secure checkout; we do not store
  your card details.

## 3. How we use information
We use the information above to operate the App, provide and personalize your
feed, deliver notifications you request, process payments, prevent fraud and
abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. How we share information
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:
- with service providers who help run the App (hosting, notifications, payments,
  and analytics) under appropriate agreements,
- when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users, or
- with your consent.

## 5. Data retention
We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to
provide the service and comply with legal obligations. When you delete your
account, we delete or anonymize your personal data in accordance with this policy.

## 6. Your rights and choices
- **Permissions:** you can control location, photo, camera, and notification
  access from your device settings.
- **Access and correction:** you can edit your profile and account details in the
  App.
- **Deletion:** you can delete your account from **Settings → Delete Account**.
  This removes your account and associated data in line with Section 5.

## 7. Children's privacy
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in
your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children.

## 8. Security
We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted transmission, to protect
your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and
we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material
changes through the App or by other reasonable means.
